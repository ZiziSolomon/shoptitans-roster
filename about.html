Assumptions & Sources

What this tool assumes about Shop Titans, where the data comes from, and how to flag corrections.

Roster rules

3+ heroes per element required. Elemental barriers appear in extreme quests, boss floors from Cinderlake Volcano onwards, and Tower of Titans Terror. A party without 3 heroes of the required element cannot pass these barriers.
2+ archetypes per element required. Tower of Titans Terror (ML67+) can apply debuffs that lock out an entire archetype group mid-run. Having only Fighters or only Rogues in an element leaves you exposed.
Spellblades count at 50% toward all elements and cannot independently satisfy an elemental requirement. A Spellblade contributes 0.5 to every element's count but will not clear a barrier on its own.

Class-specific judgements

These are the tool's opinions. Experienced players may reasonably disagree — see the feedback section below.

T1 and T2 classes need a Titan Soul to function well. Without promotion, T1/T2 heroes are significantly weaker than their promoted equivalents (Soldier → Mercenary, Cleric → Bishop, etc.). The tool flags these as requiring a Soul.
Judgement call — some players run T1/T2 unpromoted in early game.
T7 classes (Dark Knight, Velite, Chronomancer) should be avoided until the core 3-per-element roster is complete. The gold cost is extremely high for the incremental benefit.
Judgement call — late-game players may disagree.
Samurai is flagged as a difficult class. It has conflicting stat requirements (tank + damage optimisation is hard to balance) and is generally considered suboptimal until you understand the build deeply.
Judgement call — Samurai can work well with the right gear and skills.
T6 classes (Berserker, Dancer, Geomancer) unlock at ML53.
Skills become visible at hero levels 5, 10, 23, and 35.

Argon's Finest

Hire-level by points invested: 0 pts → lv1 · 1 pt → lv5 · 2 pts → lv8 · 3 pts → lv10 · 4 pts → lv13 · 5 pts → lv15 (max). At 3+ points, two skills are visible at hire, which is the key threshold for efficient retire/rehire.
Source: direct player testing. Let us know if these numbers have changed.

Hero slot unlocks

Hero slots are purchased with gold as they unlock at specific merchant levels. Maximum is 25 slots, available from ML88. All slots require gold to purchase; having the ML is necessary but not sufficient.
Source: MerchantLevel.txt community data file. Max level is ML99; no further slots exist beyond slot 25.

Something wrong? Tell us

Shop Titans updates regularly and community knowledge evolves. If you know a rule, number, or class judgement here is incorrect or outdated, please open a GitHub issue — it takes under a minute and helps everyone who uses the tool.

Especially useful: corrections to Argon's Finest levels, hero slot unlock MLs, class tier or element assignments, and any rule changes after a major game update.

SHOP TITANS — SKILL TIER LISTS BY CLASS
Source: shoptitans.dowob.dev (May 2026)
=========================================
Ratings are CLASS-SPECIFIC. The same skill can be S-tier on one class and
D-tier on another. Always check the section for the specific hero you're
evaluating.

Skill tiers: S=Essential  A=Great auxiliary  B=Usable  C=Weak  D=Avoid

Skill progression: skills level up as you equip items enchanted with the
hero's element. T1 is the base (unenchanted). T4 is fully levelled. The T4
name is shown in brackets. All tiers of a skill share the same tier rating.
Base stats shown in-game are T1 — the tracker shows T4 (max) values.

=====================================
NOTES ON UNCONFIRMED SKILL NAMES
(skills your heroes have that weren't in the tracker)
=====================================

Greater Magic Shell  — T3 form of "Mage Armor → Supreme Magic Shell".
                       Confirmed C-tier for Sorcerer, B-tier for Druid/Mage.
                       Otello (Sorcerer) has a C-tier defensive skill.

Mage Fortress        — CONFIRMED Mage Armor intermediate tier: +60% Atk, +30% Def
                       (matches Mage Armor's 2:1 ratio, ~T3 of 80/40 T4). B-tier
                       for Mage. Olga (Mage) has S+B — fails Titan Soul bar.

Magma Orb            — Almost certainly "Fireball" intermediate tier. Fireball
                       is S-tier for Druid and Sorcerer. Very likely S-tier.

Instrument Maestro   — T3/T4 of "Instrument Master". S-tier for Druid.
                       Titania's best skill. Excellent.

Lock On              — Possibly "Marksman" intermediate tier (S-tier for Ranger).
                       If confirmed Marksman family, Bartram has one good slot.

Mirror Sheen         — CONFIRMED Maintenance intermediate tier: -20% break chance,
                       +10% stats (all equip). Maintenance is the Common-rarity
                       version of Extended Warranty (same effects, smaller
                       numbers). A-tier for Ranger (Roark), C-tier for Druid
                       (Titania). Note: general guidance still rates Maintenance
                       as weak overall; only Extended Warranty (Epic) is
                       genuinely strong.

Deliverance          — CONFIRMED Smite (Wrath of the Goddess) family. T4: +75% Atk,
                       +50% Def. B-tier for Ranger (Roark).

Widowmaker           — CONFIRMED Backstab family: T1 Backstab (+30% Atk, +3% Crit)
                       → T2 Darkstalker (+40% Atk, +6% Crit) → T3 Widowmaker
                       (+60% Atk, +10% Crit). B-tier for Ninja, Wanderer, Musketeer.

Otherworldly Grace   — CONFIRMED Acrobatics family. A-tier for both Soldier
                       (Gloriana) and Thief (Sabrina). +25% Evasion at T4.

Soul Drain           — CONFIRMED T1 of Life Drain: +50% Atk, +10 HP (T4: +125% Atk,
                       +80 HP). A-tier for Sorcerer. Otello has one C-tier slot
                       (Greater Magic Shell) and one A-tier slot (Soul Drain) —
                       C+A fails Titan Soul bar. Kira is still the better Sorcerer.

Catalyst (not Master) — Working toward "Catalyst Master" (S-tier for Sorcerer,
                        Geomancer, Dancer). Kira (Sorcerer) has this — excellent.

Unbreakable          — Unknown. Appears on Calil (Soldier). Likely defensive.
                       Could be Impervious intermediate (S-tier) or Extra Plating
                       (S-tier). Probably good.

Sonic Thrust         — CONFIRMED T3 of Perforate family: T1 Perforate (+35% Atk)
                       → T2 Impale (+50% Atk) → T3 Sonic Thrust (+50% Atk, +50%
                       Crit Dmg). D-tier for Ranger, B-tier for Musketeer/Wanderer.

Kinetic Lance        — CONFIRMED T3 of Magic Darts family: T1 Magic Darts (+40% Atk)
                       → T2 Magic Missile (+60% Atk) → T3 Kinetic Lance (+80% Atk).
                       C-tier for Druid/Mage/Sorcerer. Low stats for a DPS skill.

Shrapnel Grenade     — Unknown. Appears on Selena (Spellblade). Moot — retire.

Coup de Grace        — CONFIRMED T2 of Deadly Criticals family: T1 Deadly Criticals
                       → T2 Coup de Grace (+150% Crit Dmg) → T3 Executioner (+200%
                       Crit Dmg). A-tier for Rogues (Wanderer, Musketeer, etc.).

Extra Polish         — CONFIRMED T2 of Maintenance (Common rarity): T1 Maintenance
                       → T2 Extra Polish (+10% stats, -30% Break) → T3 Mirror Sheen.
                       C-tier for Druid, D-tier for Musketeer. Weak — same family as
                       Maintenance which is generally avoid-tier.

Acrobatics note      — S-tier for Ranger, A-tier for Barbarian/Samurai, B-tier
                       for Ninja/Berserker, D-tier for Sorcerer/Mage/Druid.
                       Isadora (Samurai) has Acrobatics — B-tier, not terrible,
                       but she's a retire candidate anyway.

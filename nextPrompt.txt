## Key learnings to keep in mind

- Don't fight statistical noise. At 100k Python trials, SE on success rate
  is ~0.07-0.1pp. Don't chase <0.2pp diffs — run multiple seeds first.
- Sheet uses 50k trials, auto-drops to 5k if avg rounds > 200 (simV2.txt:1097).
- Sheet's Apps Script myFunction_V2() must be MANUALLY re-run after sheet
  edits — recalc alone doesn't update MC outputs. Stat aggregations (lizard
  total, etc) update on recalc; success rates don't. Burned 1 cycle on this.
- The CSV export from gviz endpoint reflects whatever the cells currently
  hold, so if the sheet wasn't re-run since the last edit, the script will
  silently compare against stale MC numbers. Always confirm with user.
- When swapping enchants, OTHER stats change too (HP, ATK, DEF, Crit shift
  as items/sub-stats trade off). Pull fresh from Hero Data tab via gviz
  CSV — don't trust prior PARTY values.

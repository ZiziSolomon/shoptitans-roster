SHOP TITANS - HERO SYSTEM REFERENCE
Source: st-central.net (May 2026)
====================================


## THE BIG PICTURE

Heroes quest on your behalf, fighting through dungeons and boss floors. Their
effectiveness depends on three things: class/tier, skills, and gear. The hero
system is deep — a few wrong investments early can waste weeks of progress.


## ROSTER RULES (non-negotiable foundations)

Rule 1 — 3+ heroes per element
  The six elements are: Water, Earth, Fire, Air, Light, Dark.
  You need at least three heroes of each element to handle elemental barriers
  in extreme quests, bosses from Cinderlake Volcano onwards, and Tower of
  Titans Terror difficulty.
  Note: Spellblades only count at 50% power and do NOT fulfil elemental
  requirements on their own.

Rule 2 — 2+ archetypes per element
  Each element needs heroes from at least two of the three groups:
    - Fighters (red)
    - Rogues (green)
    - Spellcasters (blue)
  This matters most for Tower of Titans Terror (ML67+), where debuffs can
  lock out entire archetype groups mid-run.


## TEAM COMPOSITION (per party)

  1 Champion
  1 Fighter (red)
  2 Rogues/Spellcasters (blue/green) — any combination
  + elements matched to the quest's barrier requirements

These are guidelines, not rigid rules, but deviating meaningfully hurts you.


## CLASSES TO AVOID UNTIL YOUR CORE ROSTER IS DONE

  - T1 and T2 classes: require a Titan Soul promotion just to function well
  - T7 classes (Dark Knight, Velites, Chronomancer): cost excessive gold
  - Spellblades: huge skill pool with mostly bad skills; rerolls are painful
  - Samurai: conflicting stat needs (tank + damage) make optimisation hard

Invest in these only after you have a solid 3-per-element roster.


## WHAT MAKES A GOOD HERO (skill evaluation)

Each hero has 3–4 skill slots. These are everything — a wasted slot is a
meaningful loss of power, so be ruthless about what you accept.

Skills to AVOID:
  - Fast Learner / Super Genius: XP bonus is useless at max level
  - Maintenance: break chance reduction is too weak to matter
    (Exception: Extended Warranty — 60% break reduction, worth keeping)
  - Smite / Parry / Mage Armor: hybrid attack+defence stats, only 120–125%
    total at rank 4; each class has better dedicated alternatives
  - Magic Darts: directly outclassed by Arcane Blast for spellcasters
  - Perforate: wastes power on Crit Damage for fighters who have only 5%
    base Crit Chance

Core principle: never fill a slot with a skill that does nothing in combat
or that has a superior alternative available to that class.


## PROMOTIONS (using Titan Souls)

What promotion does:
  - Increases base Attack, HP, and Defence
  - Unlocks T4 skills
  - Some classes: grants 4th skill slot, new equippable item types,
    upgraded innate skill, or class-specific bonuses

When to promote:
  1. The hero already has a strong skillset
  2. The hero has learned their full natural skillset
  3. They benefit significantly from promotion, OR you've already done all
     the high-benefit classes

Priority order:
  Highest  — T1/T2 (non-functional without promotion)
  High     — T3/T4 attackers (big survivability + skill slot gains)
             Ninjas and Spellblades (upgraded innate is transformative)
  Medium   — T3/T4 tanks
  Low      — T5 classes (already strong baseline)
  Lowest   — T6/T7 (best out of the box, promotion least impactful)

Champion promotion recipe: 2x Titan Soul + 1x Legendary Item (T10+) +
  5x Mythic XP Drinks + 10x Phoenix Feathers + 300x Perfect Moonstones
Champion promotion priority: Malady → King Reinhold → Ashley/Argon → others


## RETIRE / REHIRE (managing skill RNG)

The strategy: retire heroes who roll bad skills at level 5 or 10, before
you've invested heavily. This filters out bad RNG cheaply.

Key insight: early skill slots have lower odds of rare/epic skills by
default. If you use the retire/rehire method, you bypass that penalty.

Fast levelling methods:
  - Argon's Finest talent tree: hire heroes at level 10 (3 points invested),
    check first 2 skills instantly without questing
  - XP Drinks: 500 / 5,000 / 50,000 XP per drink (avoid Mythic on low levels)
  - Aurora Caves Medium (450 power) → level 5 in one run
  - Aurora Hard → Whispering Bog Hard → Barren Hard → level 10 in 3 quests


## CHAMPIONS — WHO DOES WHAT

Argon (Light)   +40% atk, +40% def. Broadly the strongest champion.
                Best for accessing hard content without maxed heroes.

Lilu (Water)    Heals 20HP/turn, +25% HP, +50% faster rest time.
                Best with high-evasion heroes; reduces downtime.

Sia (Air)       +100% components/quest, +25% atk.
                Essential for Lost City of Gold (keybearer bosses lvl 9+).

Yami (Dark)     +20% crit, +20% evasion, +50% faster questing.
                Best with crit or evasion builds; good for timed events.

Rudo (Fire)     +50% crit chance, -100% break chance (4 rounds), +50% element.
                Best with crit builds; keeps repair costs low.

Polonia (Water) 50% chance to steal super-valuable items, +25% def, +10% eva.
                Strong early; shifts to raising item drop tiers post-T12.

Donovan (Fire)  +14% atk per blue hero, +10% HP per red, +7% crit/eva per green.
                Tailored buffs; good for mixed-composition parties.

Hemma (Dark)    Drains 7% HP from one hero/turn, heals herself +25HP, +35% atk,
                +25% HP for party (net positive).
                Reliable late-game DPS + party buffer.

Ashley (Earth)  +25% atk & def, +20% faster quests; doubled vs bosses.
                Best for boss content and Dragon Invasion events.

Bjorn (Air)     +30% atk, +20% HP, +50% crit dmg; doubled in flash quests;
                grants one extra flash quest attempt.
                Strong in flash quests and crit-focused teams.

Malady (Light)  +30% atk, +15% evasion, +10% crit; earns enchantments as loot.
                Pairs with green heroes; must survive for loot to trigger.

Reinhold (Gold) +30% atk, +30% def, +30% HP; finds unusual items IF he survives.
                Needs a tank; worth running for unique loot collection.
                Event-only champion — appears roughly one week per month. Hire
                when available.

Tamas (Earth)   Up to +200% random atk bonus; finds Leather bonus loot.
                Exceptional DPS ceiling but needs support to survive.
                Event-only champion — appears roughly one week per year. Rare
                window; prioritise hiring when available.

Champion-hero evasion pairings:
  95% evasion hero → Lilu
  85% evasion hero → Donovan, Malady, or Polonia
  75% evasion hero → Yami (adds +20%)

Fighter pairing notes:
  Offensive fighters → Argon / Ashley / Bjorn / Rudo (damage reduction)
  Crit fighters       → Rudo / Bjorn (skill slot constraints make crit focus easier)
